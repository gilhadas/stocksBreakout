## 🔍 Deep Dive: Why Strategy Underperformed

### 1. Bull Market Disadvantage
Breakout strategies are designed to:
- Capture momentum moves
- Protect downside with stops
- **Exit when trend breaks**

In a steady bull market:
- Few big "breakout" moves
- Steady grind upward
- Getting stopped out of winning trends
- Missing the full ride up

### 2. Opportunity Cost
Each trade risks only 1% of capital but caps at 10% position size:
- Conservative sizing = less exposure
- SPY = 100% exposure always
- In bull markets, more exposure = more gains

### 3. Time in Market
- SPY: 100% invested at all times
- Strategy: Selectively invested (51 trades over 2 years)
- Cash drag when not in positions
